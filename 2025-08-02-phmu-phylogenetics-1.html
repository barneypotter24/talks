Some notation

  • $P(X)$: probability of $X$
  • $P(X|Y)$: conditional probability of $X$ given $Y$
  • $\theta$: model parameters
  • $\mathcal{L}(\theta|X)$: likelihood of $\theta$ given data $X$


$\mathcal{L}(\theta | X) = P(X | \theta)$
