Tuberculosis

  • Slow developing, primarily respiratory wasting disease,
  • Aerosol droplet transmission,
  • Most deadly infectious disease in the (history of the) world,
  • Infected humans for millennia.
Source: Klafubra via Wikipedia
  • 10-20 per cent of cases are extramulmonary
  • Even today TB causes over 10million new infections and over 1million deaths annually
  • Responsible for up to a quarter of deaths in pre-antibiotic Europe!
    • Mention: very effectively treated by antibiotics
    • Unfortunately: has a tendency to develop drug resistance
Source: Our World in Data
Most of the burden of disease falls on less economically developed countries that cannot afford access to second and third line antibiotics.

Mycobacterium tuberculosis

  • Aerobic, slow-growing bacteria,
  • Does not undergo recombination or contain a large accessory genome,
  • 4.4 mebabase genome.

M.tuberculosis in Moldova

  • Countrywide analysis of ALL culture-positive M.tb cases in Moldova (2018-2019).
  • 2770 diagnosed, 1834 complete genomes.
  • Two major sublineages in the country: Beijing (2.2.1), Ural (4.2).

The game plan

  1. Maximum likelihood phylogenetic inference;
  2. Time-calibrate the phylogeny:
    • Fixed clock model,
    • 0.5 SNPs/genome/year;
  3. Infer geographic spread:

Conclusions

  • A clade of M.tb Ural lineage 4.2 arose in the last 50 years,
  • This clade appears highly fit, and has acquired numerous drug-resistance conferring mutations,
  • EC1 may have its origins in Russia, or another former Soviet Republic.

What's next?

  • Retrospective sequncing of Moldovan tuberculosis cases going back to 2004,
  • Bayesian phylogenetic/phylogeographic inference,
  • Incorporate a more unbiased sampling of the regional burden of tuberculosis.

baltic is a Python library based on matplotlib for the parsing and visualization of phylogenetic trees and their associated analyses.

Key features of baltic

  1. Simple API,
  2. Efficient parsing and visualization of large tree files,
  3. Full suite of tree manipulation features,
  4. Low-level implementation for power users,
  5. High-level function wrapping,
  6. Minimalist default design choice.

Plotting the n=20 tree

Tree manipulation features

  • Automated MRCA search,
  • Rerooting (algorithmic or manual),
  • Metadata integration,
  • Clade collpse, reticulation, and tree abstraction,
  • Posterior distribution summariazation.
